
Nordland Caption Failure Annotation Instructions

Task:
You are reviewing cases where the language/caption-based retrieval signal selected the wrong location.
For each case, assign one failure type.

Allowed labels:

A_generic_non_place_discriminative
Use this when the captions are plausible scene descriptions, but they are too generic to distinguish the true place from the wrong retrieved place.
Example: both descriptions mention train tracks, snow, mountains, forest, bridge, road, etc., without unique place-specific details.

B_caption_artifact_or_degenerate
Use this when the caption appears to be affected by a visual/captioning artifact, such as black screen, white text, unreadable frame, blank frame, or otherwise degenerate/uninformative caption.

C_other_or_unclear
Use this when neither A nor B clearly applies, or the case is ambiguous.

Confidence:
1 = low confidence
2 = medium confidence
3 = high confidence

Please fill only:
- second_annotator_failure_type
- second_annotator_confidence_1_to_3
- second_annotator_notes

Do not change case_id or other columns.
